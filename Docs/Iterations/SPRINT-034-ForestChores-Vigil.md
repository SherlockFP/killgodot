## SPRINT-034a — Four forest chores as data (S)
**Objective (visible):** matches deal four new forest chores: **Gather deadwood** (3 piles → bundle → camp woodpile),
**Pitch camp** (a two-person canvas → 3 pegs → tent), **Light the trail lanterns** (taper → 5 lanterns → back),
**Gather herbs** (3 clusters → church table).

### Acceptance (fixed)
1. The 4 chores are in `Tools/Level/morrowmere_world_chores.json` (generated by `gen_world_chores.py`);
   `kg.WorldChore.Routes` finds every step reachable on the navmesh; scripted-player times 45–90 s; the long-chore
   class is dealt at most once per player (a `FKGWorldChoreRules::Deal` test).
2. **Lint [L]** against `morrowmere_forest_v2.json`: no dealt step lies in the Deep band; each variant's continuous
   Middle-band time is ≤ 20 s by day. Herb clusters exist only in Edge/Middle; herbs go only to the church table
   (no pocket herb item).
3. **Fake parity:** an Impatient doing a forest chore produces the same world effect (logs stacked, lanterns lit, tent
   up) and adds 0 to the bar. The chore smoke is extended with one forest chore end to end across two processes.
4. Offscreen shots: the lit lantern line seen from the village at night; the pitched tent. `run_invariants.ps1` passes.

### Writable scope
- `Tools/Level/morrowmere_world_chores.json`, `Tools/Level/gen_world_chores.py` (data + lint only)
- `Chores/WorldChores/` only if a new world-item definition (Canvas, Herb basket) cannot be expressed as data
- tests, the forest design doc §7

### Limits
- 3 fix attempts per check; 2 feel-and-look rounds per chore; plateau stop.
- Tasks are dealt once per match today (`KGGameMode.cpp:764`); lanterns, woodpile and fire reset at dawn as **world
  state**, not as re-dealt chores. Daily re-dealing is out of scope (proposal).
- Needs user approval O4 (the 45–90 s "long chore" exception to SPRINT-016's 30–75 s rule).

**Depends on:** SPRINT-016 finished, 033a.

## SPRINT-034b — Campfire, torch/tinder/resin, Camp Vigil (M)
**Objective (visible):** the camp fire can be lit, fed, doused or kicked apart by anyone, and it glows over the village
at night. From night 2, 2–4 players who signed the vigil book in the day walk to the camp after the meeting and keep
the fire alive until dawn. Success adds a small, side-blind chunk to the Readiness bar.

### Acceptance (fixed)
1. **Test** `KillGodot.Forest.Vigil`, ≥ 12 cases: min 2 / max 4 signatures (max 2 at N ≤ 7); arrival window
   max(0.55 × Night, 60 s); ≤ 15 s total outside the 12 m ring; fire lit ≥ 90% of the night after the window;
   B / night cap / match cap = 1/1/2 at N = 6, 1/2/4 at N = 12, 2/3/6 at N = 20; **side-blind** (an Impatient signer
   counts exactly like a Town signer); the bar never exceeds what living Town players could still reach with their own
   tasks; night 1 closed; no gold reward.
2. **Test:** fuel 100, −2.5/s, a log +50; safe radius 10 m, 6 m under 20 fuel; bucket douse → wet ash, kick 3 s →
   scattered embers, empty → cold ash (ash type kept until dawn); a torch relights only with 1 Resin at a lit campfire;
   tinder box 3 per night; torch box 1 per player per day.
3. **Two-process smoke:** A lights the fire → B sees it lit; A forced Impatient lights it → lit for B too (side-blind);
   a bucket douse → wet ash on both ends.
4. **[U]** bots sign (Town and Impatient bots with the same chance), feed below 40 fuel, and log `KG_VIGIL`; the
   success rate is reported against F5 (60–85%, report only).
5. **Data lint:** the camp is ≤ 110 m navmesh path from the square and ≥ 15 m from a village lane. Shots: the camp fire
   seen from the village at night; the vigil HUD line. `run_invariants.ps1` passes.

### Writable scope
- `Forest/` (new `AKGCampfire`, vigil book, vigil rules)
- `Inventory/KGItemCatalog.cpp` (Torch, Tinder, Resin)
- `Core/KGGameMode.cpp`: vigil reward hook only, serial
- `AI/KGBotController.cpp`: vigil behaviour only, serial
- `UI/Forest/`, tests

### Limits
- 3 fix attempts per check; 2 look rounds for the fire; plateau stop; header changes in one user-timed batch.
- The vigil book is never mirrored to the Town Board. No new currency sinks.
- Out of scope → proposals: Signal Fire, Liar's Dice at the campfire, the Watchman curfew exemption (Roadmap 028),
  the Night Journal cost (027a).

**Depends on:** 034a, 033b, 035b; real matches after Roadmap 020b.
