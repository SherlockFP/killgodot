## 1. Vision

Morrowmere is a **theatre whose stage is the harbour**. From anywhere in town the ground falls toward a round stone basin, and the basin's mouth frames a shrine island. The composition has three layers.

1. **One axis.** The Processional Axis runs from the pagoda (45,150) to the bell tower (−5.6,−61.4). Its direction inland is (−0.2328, −0.9725). The line strings together the sea torii, the harbour mouth, the Long Jetty, the Grand Stair, the fountain, the Scala, the Pilgrim Stair, the Belvedere and the bell tower. Walk it seaward and the island grows in the frame; walk it inland and the bell tower does.
2. **Rings.** Terraces are rings around the basin centre O = (24.89, 66.0), which lies on the axis.
   - Ring streets are the contours.
   - Stairs, opes and ramps run down the radials.
   - Houses stand shoulder to shoulder in straight party-wall blocks that follow the rings, so every street is a wall of facades, not a lawn with boxes on it.
3. **Two wings of looser land.** In the west, the Brookside valley has the brook, smithy, mill and woods. In the east, the Orchard Upland has walled fields and the windmill, and Lighthouse Point closes the cove.

The flanks are organic on purpose. They make the formal centre read as the heart.

The fix for "soulless, side by side" is structural, not decorative:
- **Figure / ground:** continuous street walls, walled back gardens, one enclosed square.
- **Hierarchy:** main rings, lanes, opes; civic buildings at the ends of views.
- **Elevation:** four levels, each reached by a designed stair.
- **Framed views:** 7 sightlines, all checked in 3D.

## 3. The heart: Fountain Square (z 5)

- **Shape:** a fan-shaped room about 22 × 31 m (`squares[fountain_square]`, 659 m²). It is widest at the Town Hall end and narrowest at the sea, like a small Campo. Its south edge is the **balustrade over the Grand Stair**. That is the only window to the sea: the Harbour Row roofs, the basin, the mouth and the island all line up on the axis.
- **Enclosure on all four sides:**
  - **West:** Bakery (C03, 6×6), then the gable ends of the Lip and Mid rows. Rope Walk enters here.
  - **East:** Inn "The Latecomer" (C04, 6×8, 3 storeys), then the Lip/Mid gables. Market Street enters here.
  - **North:** Town Hall (C05, 8×8, 3 storeys), the Scala between the hall and the Clock Tower (C06, 4×4, 4 levels, ladder, top 24.5 m), and the Guild House.
  - **South:** the balustrade, with the Grand Stair cut into it.

  The ring streets enter at the flanks, so no street looks straight through the square.
- **Centre:** the fountain (`SM_KG_Fountain`) at (9.98, 3.76), on the axis, with **20 player starts on a 7.5 m ring** facing it.
- **Triangle of things to look at:**
  - Moot Stage + gallows (`SM_KG_Stage`, tag `KG_Gallows`) at (1.61, −3.29), in front of the Town Hall steps. Everyone faces the hall. The notice board stands at (3.64, −7.27).
  - Dead Tree at (18.29, 10.51), a black silhouette against the sea window.
  - Market stalls along the east flank at (17.3, −0.05).
- **Chores in and around the square:** PostNotice, FileReports, BakeBread, PourAle and StockStall. These are the short, safe, witnessed jobs.

## 8. Gameplay flow

- **Meeting place:** the fountain square, with the Moot Stage and gallows. It is the geometric hub of the rings.
- **Run times** from the fountain, measured over the real walk graph (5.8 m/s, stairs ×1.18, ladder time added):
  - Every **home door reaches the square in 4.8–23.2 s**. The median is about 9 s; the farthest home is the farmhouse H20.
  - The **farthest walkable point** is the mill-pond end of the Woods Path, at 32 s.
  - The square is therefore reachable in about 20–30 s from anywhere, which meets the Docs/04 cap.
- **Home allocation** for games under 20 players: fill H01–H09 (Heart) first, then H10–H12 (quay) and H13–H16 (upper), and H17–H20 last. Unassigned homes stay locked "empty houses" with loot, per Docs/04.
- **Risk gradient:**
  - Safe: the square and the quay (witnessed, lit, open).
  - Medium: the rings and the gardens.
  - Dangerous: the opes and the Tide Alley (dark, 2.3–2.4 m wide, arches), the Woods Path and Hollow Way, the Headland Road, the mole and the tower tops.
- **Hiding:** walled back gardens (with sheds and water butts, from dressing), the arches, the cellars below the Harbour Wall (future), the catacombs under the mausoleum (future), and the well cellar (future).
- **Perches (Watcher roles):** the Belvedere, the Clock Tower top, the Balcony Lane parapet, the lighthouse gallery.

## 11. Implementation plan (ordered; each step shows new content on screen)

### 11.1 `Tools/Blender/kg_build_terrain.py` (terrain v2)

1. **Input:** read `Tools/Level/morrowmere_layout_v2.json`. Keep a `LAYOUT_FILE` constant so v1 stays one line away. Convert to Blender with (x, −y) at the end only; do all maths in UE metres.
2. **Two grids, one watertight surface:**
   - **Core:** x −130…130, y −125…125 (UE), step **0.5 m** (521 × 501 verts). Hard terrace edges need a 0.5 m band.
   - **Outer:** the existing 640 m at 2.5 m step, with faces fully inside the core rectangle dropped.

   The core bounds are multiples of 2.5 m, and both grids sample the same `height()`, so the seam matches. Export both objects in one GLB (`SM_KG_Terrain_Core`, `SM_KG_Terrain_Outer`), and enable Nanite on import if the project allows it. Write `KG_Terrain_heights.json` as `{size, step, n, heights, core: {x0, y0, step, nx, ny, heights}}`. `ground()` in the builder and in `kg_dress_common` samples the core first.
3. **Vectorised geometry with Blender's bundled numpy:**
   - point-in-polygon with even-odd over the polygon edges, vectorised across all vertices;
   - distance to a polyline, vectorised per segment.

   These are about 40 M numpy operations, which takes seconds.
4. **`height(x, y)`, in priority order:**
   1. **Stairs and ramps:** inside `width/2 + 0.3` of the centreline, z is linear from z0 to z1 along the run. For kit stairs, use the stair's hidden-ramp line from the foot riser to the top riser.
   2. **Flat terraces** (`z` set): inside the polygon, z = terrace z.
   3. **Slope terraces** (`z_profile`): inside the polygon, z = interp(stops, (p − origin)·dir). Then add `mounds[]` (smoothstep bump).
   4. **Hard edges** (terrace boundaries carrying a `retaining_walls[]` run or a `cliffs[]` line): no blend. The lower terrace keeps its z up to the boundary, so the step sits in the 0.5 m band behind the wall. **Soft edges** (`edge: soft`, `soft_m`): outside the polygon, blend z toward the neighbour or natural value over `soft_m` with smoothstep.
   5. **Natural land** (land that is in no terrace): the existing meadow noise + mountain ring, **minus the plateau pull** (delete `PLATEAU`, `lane_factor` flattening and `pad_height`).
   6. **Coast:** outside `coast.land_polygon`, z = lerp(−3 → −14) with distance from the coast over `far_at_m` (120 m). Within 6 m inside the land and outside terraces, z blends from `beach_z` 0.8 up to the natural value. **Delete the y-only `coast = smooth(-34, -70, y)`.**
   7. **Basin:** within `basin.radius` of O and outside land, z = `floor_z` (−3).
   8. **Mole:** within `mole.walk_width/2` of the polyline, z = 2.5. Out to `width/2`, a 1:1 armour slope down to the seabed (rock colour).
   9. **Stream:** within `width/2`, z = bed z interpolated along the polyline. From `width/2` to `width/2 + 2.5`, a smoothstep bank back to the surrounding z. Ponds: z = pond z − 0.8 inside the radius.
5. **Vertex colours:**

   | Surface | Colour |
   |---|---|
   | flat terraces | `COBBLE` on lanes / squares / stairs (lane polygons from `lanes[]` + `squares[]`), else short grass |
   | quay, mole, stair corridors | `ROCK` (stone flags) |
   | slope terraces | grass, with `PATH` on lanes |
   | slopes over 0.9 and cliffs | rock |
   | beach | sand |
   | stream bed | wet sand |

   Keep the palette constants.
6. **Log check:** `KG_TERRAIN` prints terrace z samples at every stair end, which must equal z0/z1.

## 13. Risks and notes

- **0.5 m core grid** is about 260 k verts. Enable Nanite on the core mesh, or split it into four tiles for culling. If performance forces 1 m, move the retaining walls 0.6 m toward the low side.
- **The fan tilts 13.5°** because the axis aims at the fixed island. Everything is authored in the polar frame (`axis.origin_O`, `dir_inland`), so a later change of the island or the axis is one constant in `author_layout_v2.py`.
- **Kit stairs are 25.7°.** Walkable, but steep for combat. The hidden ramps keep bots and ragdolls clean. The wide axis stairs have landings every 1 m of rise.
- **Density vs witness gameplay.** The square, the quay and Balcony Lane stay open and lit. The opes and gardens are the dark places. Test a 20-bot meeting call early.
- **Dressing rewrite cost.** The zone modules hold v1 literals. Migrate to anchors module by module; `zone_of` polygons already partition the map.
