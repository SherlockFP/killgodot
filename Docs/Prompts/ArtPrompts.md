# Sanat üretim promptları

> **Bu oturumda** fikir üretimi `Tools/Blender/kg_variant_sheet.py` (prosedürel, tohumlu varyasyon sayfaları) ile yapılıyor.
> Harici bir AI görsel ya da 3D aracı kullanırsan aşağıdaki promptlar hazır. Stil anahtarı her promptta aynı kalsın ki sonuçlar tutarlı olsun.

**Stil anahtarı (her prompta ekle):**
`stylized 3D toy-like render, painted wooden puppet characters with hinged nutcracker jaws, bold black outlines, cel shading with warm-to-cool shadows (TF2 illustrative lighting), vibrant saturated palette (turquoise sea, red and orange roofs, mustard, teal), clean simple shapes, low-poly carved facets, Among Us 3D meets Team Fortress 2, colonial fishing village 1690s`

## Konsept görseller
1. **Köy genel görünüm:** `aerial view of Morrowmere, a colonial fishing village in a bay surrounded by green meadows and mountains, lighthouse on a cliff, wooden docks with rowboats, town square with a leafless dead tree and gallows, sea fog wall on the horizon, golden hour, <stil anahtarı>`
2. **Gece:** `the same village at night, deep indigo sky, full moon, warm orange windows, lighthouse beam sweeping through lilac fog, a masked figure with a clock-face mask in an alley, <stil anahtarı>`
3. **Meyhane lobisi:** `interior of 'The Late Arrival' tavern, long wooden table with candles, piano, dart board, puppets laughing and arguing, cozy, <stil anahtarı>`
4. **Ana menü sahnesi:** `a puppet sitting at the end of a pier fishing at dusk, lighthouse beam, huge pastel clouds, calm turquoise sea, cinematic wide shot, <stil anahtarı>`

## Karakterler (Yüzler)
`character turnaround sheet, front / 3-4 / side / back, a painted wooden puppet villager named <İsim>: <imza detayları>, hinged jaw slightly open, big expressive eyes, T-pose, plain background, <stil anahtarı>`

Örnek imza detayları: `freckles, short ginger hair, missing front tooth, teal fisher sweater` · `bowler hat, walrus mustache, red nose, burgundy coat, gold pocket watch` · `monocle, top hat, crimson scarf, sinister smile, umbrella`

## Rol kartları (tarot)
`tarot card illustration for the role '<Rol>' in a social deduction game, ornate brass and parchment frame, puppet character performing <yetenek>, symbolic props, <taraf rengi: town = warm gold, impatient = crimson, neutral = violet>, <stil anahtarı>`

## Silah ve yakın dövüş skinleri
`first-person viewmodel concept, gloved puppet hand holding a <eşya: umbrella blade / clock-hand dagger / frying pan / swordfish>, CS2-style composition lower right, rarity glow <renk>, <stil anahtarı>`

## Logo
`game logo 'KILL GODOT', hand-lettered wooden sign letters, one 'O' replaced by a cracked clock face with the minute hand as a dagger, dark comedy, vibrant, transparent background`

## 3D üretim (Meshy / Tripo / Rodin gibi araçlar için)
- `low-poly stylized wooden barrel with dark iron hoops, game-ready, under 600 triangles, flat colours`
- `low-poly colonial two-story timber-frame house, red roof, cream walls, flower boxes, modular, game-ready`
- `stylized swordfish melee weapon, chunky, toy-like, under 2000 triangles`
